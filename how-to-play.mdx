This page covers everything you need to know to play Santa Run — from opening the game in your browser to understanding the jump arc well enough to clear every obstacle in your path.

## Starting the Game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open index.html in your browser">
    Navigate to the `Santa-Run/` folder and open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser. No local server, no build step, and no internet connection are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Santa's Idle animation">
    Once the page loads, Santa appears on the left side of the screen cycling through his 16-frame **Idle** animation. The background is static and the score reads **0**. The game is waiting for your input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Enter to begin">
    Press **Enter** to start the run. Santa immediately transitions to his 11-frame **Run** animation, the background begins scrolling left, flame obstacles start spawning from the right, and the score counter begins incrementing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action | When it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter** | Start the game | Only when the game is stopped (not already running) |
| **Space** | Jump | Only while Santa is running (not while already in the air) |

<Note>
  Both keys are detected on `keyup`, not `keydown`. Hold timing in mind: the action fires the moment you **release** the key, not when you first press it.
</Note>

## Objective

Survive as long as possible by jumping over the flame obstacles that scroll in from the right side of the screen. There are no levels, no speed increases, and no power-ups — pure endurance.

Your **score increments by 1 every 100ms** you stay alive. The score is entirely time-based, so every jump you land successfully is worth more time and therefore more points.

## Jumping

When you press **Space**, Santa's Run animation stops and his 16-frame Jump animation begins. The jump arc works as follows:

- **Frames 1–9 (ascent):** Santa moves **up 20px per frame**, rising a total of **160px** above the ground.
- **Frames 10–16 (descent):** Santa moves **down 20px per frame**, returning to his starting `marginTop` of **580px**.

Each frame lasts 100ms, giving the full jump arc a total air time of approximately **1.6 seconds**. Once frame 16 completes, the jump interval clears itself, Santa switches back to the Run animation, and the run sound resumes.

<Info>
  Santa starts and lands at a `marginTop` of **580px**. During peak altitude (after frame 9), his `marginTop` is **420px** — well above the collision zone.
</Info>

## Game Over

Collision detection runs inside the `moveBlocks` interval (every 100ms). A collision is registered when **both** of these conditions are true at the same moment:

- A flame block's `marginLeft` is between **18px and 118px** (the 100px-wide danger window in front of Santa)
- Santa's `marginTop` is between **520px and 580px** (i.e., Santa is on or near the ground — not airborne)

When a collision is detected:

1. All active intervals — running, jumping, background scrolling, scoring, spawning, and block movement — are immediately cleared.
2. The **Dead** animation plays through all 17 frames (one per 100ms, ~1.7 seconds total).
3. After frame 17, Santa's `marginTop` is reset to `580px` and the **Game Over** overlay becomes visible, displaying the text "Game Over", "Your Score", and your final score value.

<Warning>
  There is no iframe — collision detection checks every single active block on each tick. The longer you survive, the more blocks accumulate in the DOM, which means more checks per tick.
</Warning>

## Restarting

Click the **Try Again** button on the Game Over overlay. It calls `location.reload()`, which performs a full browser page reload — resetting all JavaScript variables, clearing all intervals, removing all spawned DOM elements, and returning Santa to his Idle animation.

There is no partial reset or state restore; every new run starts completely fresh.

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Jump early.** The jump arc lasts 1.6 seconds and Santa only rises during the first 9 frames. If you wait until a flame is visually close, you will still be on the ground when it reaches the collision zone. Press **Space** as soon as you see an obstacle enter the screen.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Survival is everything.** The score is purely time-based — there are no bonus points for style, close calls, or streaks. Every 100ms you stay alive counts equally, so a cautious early jump beats a perfectly timed late one every time.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **You can't jump mid-air.** `jumpWorkerId` must be `0` for Space to register a jump. If you press Space while already airborne, nothing happens. Plan one jump per obstacle.
</Tip>
